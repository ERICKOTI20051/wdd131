Savanna Trails Kenya

WDD 131 Site Plan

Site Name

Savanna Trails Kenya

This name tells visitors the site is about safari travel in Kenya. "Trails" suggests guided routes and adventure, and "Savanna" names the landscape people come to see.

Optional domain availability: savannatrails.ke

Site Purpose

The site helps first-time travelers plan a safari. It provides destination guides, wildlife and best-season information, packing tips, and a trip inquiry form.

Scenarios

  • When is the best time to see the Great Migration in the Maasai Mara?
  • What should I pack for a three-day safari?
  • Which national park is best for seeing elephants?
  • How can I ask about a trip for my family?

Color Schema

  • Acacia Green #1F3D2B: header, footer, headings
  • Savanna Gold #E0B04A: accents, borders, buttons
  • Sunset Orange #B5521B: links and calls to action
  • Sage Mist #EEF1E6: page background
  • Earth Charcoal #262620: paragraph text

Typography

  • Playfair Display: headings and the site name, giving an expedition-journal feel.
  • Open Sans: paragraphs, navigation, and forms, chosen for readability on small screens.

Wireframes

Home page layout in mobile and desktop views.

Planned Pages

  • Home: hero, featured parks, and tips
  • Destinations: park cards with filters, built with JavaScript
  • Plan Your Safari: inquiry form that saves details with localStorage
  • References: image and text sources
